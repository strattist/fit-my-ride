Fit my Ride — UX Design Validation Report

DESIGN.md + EXPERIENCE.md · _bmad-output/planning-artifacts/ux-designs/ux-to-choose-2026-09-28/

This report documents findings as originally raised, each now marked with its resolution status. Two reviewers ran in parallel against the finalized spine pair: the rubric walker (structural/mechanical coverage against the BMad DESIGN.md/EXPERIENCE.md spec) and an ad-hoc accessibility reviewer (dispatched because the product has an explicit colorblind-safety requirement). Neither reviewer re-ran after fixes landed — findings below are followed by what was actually done, not a re-graded verdict.

Rubric walker: a well-executed fast-path spine pair — canonical shapes followed exactly, every token resolves, both PRD journeys have full Key Flows with climax and failure paths. Gaps were narrow and concrete: two components with behavior but no visual spec, one broken cross-reference, one worked mock never linked. 11 findings (0 critical, 3 high, 3 medium, 5 low) — all 11 resolved in this pass.

Accessibility: the colorblind-safety approach was structurally sound (blue↔orange scale, not red/green; glyph+number+color triple redundancy) but two real gaps needed fixing: the focus ring failed contrast against most heat-tier fills despite the spine's own claim otherwise, and the Geometry Diagram had no accessible text alternative. 10 findings (0 critical, 2 high, 5 medium, 3 low) — 9 of 10 resolved; one low-severity item (thin contrast margin on one heat-tier's text) was acknowledged and flagged for a post-build re-check rather than changed, to avoid tuning a color on an untested assumption about anti-aliased rendering.

A subsequent editorial pass (structure + prose lenses, `bmad-review`) found the accessibility fixes had introduced real redundancy — the same rationale restated 2–3 times across sections — plus 23 sentence-level issues (grammar, terminology consistency, a genuine column-count arithmetic error). All structure findings and all clear-cut prose fixes were applied; sentence-style heading capitalization (Microsoft Writing Style Guide) was flagged but not applied — a ~20-heading rename judged out of proportion for a hobby-project spine, noted here rather than silently skipped.

Accessibility review

review-accessibility.md

Structural bet was right (blue↔orange, not red/green; triple-redundant encoding; explicit "never color alone" rule). Two real gaps needed fixing before build.

HighResolved

Focus ring not visible against most heat-tier fills, contradicting the spine's own claim

focus-ring vs. 4 of 5 heat fills, computed: 1.10–2.02:1 (fail), 3.14:1 (thin pass)
EXPERIENCE.md claimed the ring "stays visible against every fill" — the numbers said otherwise.
Fix applied: replaced the single ring on heat cells with a two-tone halo (inner ring in background, outer ring in focus-ring) — verified against all 5 fills regardless of which sits underneath. New focus-halo-inner/focus-halo-outer tokens; single ring kept for every other control (already high-contrast there).
HighResolved

Geometry Diagram had no accessible text alternative (WCAG 1.1.1)

Inlined SVG, no title/desc/role/aria-label
The diagram's real job — which line is which parameter — is a purely spatial answer a screen-reader user got zero equivalent of.
Fix applied: added role="img" + summary aria-label to the mock's inlined SVG; documented as an explicit spine decision in Accessibility Floor (column headers' full names are the treated-as-sufficient substitute for the spatial mapping, not an oversight).
MediumResolved

Colorblind-simulation claim asserted, not evidenced

DESIGN.md "ΔE ≥ 27" — no simulation output existed anywhere in the workspace
Fix applied: re-ran the CVD simulation (Machado et al. 2009 matrices) and saved method + full results to .working/heat-scale-cvd-check.md. The real worst-case figure is ΔE ≈ 23, not 27 — DESIGN.md's claim was corrected to match the evidence rather than kept as a rounder, unverified number.
MediumResolved

cell-unknown-border fails non-text contrast against its own background

#4A4760 vs. surface #1E1D27 ≈ 1.87:1 (needs 3:1)
Fix applied: lightened to #726F8A (≈3.5:1, computed).
MediumResolved
